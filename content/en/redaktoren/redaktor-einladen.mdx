An editor ("Redaktor") can record games live for the teams assigned to them — kickoff, goals, cards, score. Only the club admin can invite editors and manage their teams.

<Callout type="info">
The club admin themselves doesn't need an invitation — they're automatically allowed to manage every team in the club.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Editors">

In the dashboard under **Editors**, you'll see all existing editors and any pending invitations.

</Step>
<Step title="Create an invitation">

Optionally enter the person's email address and choose which team(s) they should be responsible for.

</Step>
<Step title="Share the link">

You'll get an invitation link — copy it and send it to the person directly (WhatsApp, email, whatever works).

</Step>
<Step title="They set their password">

Via the link, the person sets their own password directly — no detour through a generic registration page.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing teams later

You don't need a new invitation for this. Click **Edit** next to the person, re-select their teams, and save.

<Callout type="tip">
An editor can be responsible for several teams at once — even across different sports, if your club offers more than one.
</Callout>

## Removing an editor

Click **Remove** next to the person. Their LiveClub account itself stays intact — they just lose access to your club.

<Callout type="warning" title="Game in progress?">
Don't remove an editor in the middle of a game they're administering — the game is left without an active editor until someone takes it over.
</Callout>
