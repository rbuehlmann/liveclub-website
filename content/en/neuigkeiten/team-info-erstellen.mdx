A Team Info is a short message from your team that appears immediately in the app's feed — between games, with no draft state, images, or comments. Both the club admin and reporters (for their assigned teams) can post one.

<Steps>
<Step title="Go to Team Info">

In the dashboard, under **Team Info**, you'll find the "New team info" form.

</Step>
<Step title="Enter a title and text">

Both fields have a character limit — the form shows you the exact maximum. There's deliberately no image upload and no comments, just short text.

</Step>
<Step title="Optionally add a push notification">

The "Send as push notification" toggle is independent of the team info itself — leave it off and the message appears silently in the feed, with no one notified.

</Step>
<Step title="Publish">

Without push, the button reads "Publish"; with the toggle on, it reads "Publish & send". With push enabled, you'll first see a confirmation with the usage rules (see below).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Daily limit for team infos and pushes

Both the number of team infos per day and how many of those may additionally be sent as a push are limited — the form always shows how much you have left for today. Once the team info limit is reached, the form can't be submitted at all; if only the push quota is used up, you can still publish, just without a push.

<Callout type="info" title="Why a limit?">
The push quota protects your followers from too many notifications — a team that pushes several times a day quickly leads fans to turn off app notifications entirely.
</Callout>

## Before you send a push notification

A push goes out immediately and is visible to every follower of your team — unlike the silent feed entry, it can't be taken back. Only send relevant, team-related information. In particular, advertising/spam, unlawful or offensive content, and content that infringes third-party rights are not allowed.

<Callout type="warning">
As the sender, you're responsible for the content of every push notification you send.
</Callout>

## Hiding a team info again

"Hide" instantly removes an already-published team info from the app's feed. This isn't a permanent deletion — the message stays in your dashboard, just no longer publicly visible. A push that was already sent can't be recalled this way.
