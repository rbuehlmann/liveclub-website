Before you can create teams, editors, or games, your club needs its own account on LiveClub. This is done entirely through the website — no app installation required.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the registration page">

Go to [liveclub.app/register](/register) and choose "Register club".

</Step>
<Step title="Create an account">

Enter your name, email address, and a password, and accept the terms of service.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in club details">

Club name, sport, country, plus a contact name and contact email. The sport can't be changed later, so choose it deliberately.

</Step>
<Step title="Done">

Your club is ready to go immediately, including a 30-day free trial.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip">
You can add your logo and club colors any time later under **Club → Edit** in the dashboard — they don't need to be finished at registration.
</Callout>

## What happens after registering?

You land directly in the club dashboard. From there you can:

- Create teams
- Invite editors
- Create games

## Trial and beyond

<Callout type="info" title="30 days free">
Every new club automatically starts with a 30-day free trial — no payment details needed. After that, you can choose a team-size tier directly in the dashboard.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change the sport later?**
No, that's deliberate — decide during registration.

**Do I need a credit card for the trial?**
No, the 30 days are completely free and require no payment details.
